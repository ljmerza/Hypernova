# Hypernova design system

The base comes from 0stems (atmosphere, colour, type), the structure from
Hyperscribe (the seam, pills, quiet copy), and urgent states from
Hypernavigate (the filled block). It's implemented in `android/sharedui` (the tokens, the marks, the kit and the chat, for both apps) and
`android/app/src/main/java/com/prudnikweb/hypernova/ui/` (the rest of the phone). The home screen
widgets repeat them as Android resources. (The Mac app's SwiftUI port, "Mac menu bar and mini app",
and Lens, "The Lens guide" and "Lens (G2)", are no longer in this repository; those sections stay
as history.)

## Colour (Atlas, owner, 2026-11-01)

A few named tokens (`theme/Tokens.kt`, `Palette`, `AtlasColors`), and everything else derived from them in `HN`, so a theme selector (LATER L109) swaps one value. The widget, the terminal and the Mermaid pages can't read Kotlin and repeat the values as text.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| space | `#060A17` | The page: near-black navy |
| ink | `#F1E9D6` | Text, the chart's lines, the logo |
| dim | `#8E8C80` | Quiet text |
| line | `#252A3A` | Hairlines |
| live | `#6FE0C2` | Verdigris: **working**, primary, success |
| ask | `#FFA63C` | Amber: **needs you** (approvals, waiting) |
| stop | `#EE4468` | Limits, errors, deny, failed exits |
| idle | `#7E7D74` | An idle mark |
| light | `#E7DAB8` | The chart's stipple |
| glow | `#F8F0DC` | The brightest highlight (only in `accentBright`) |

Derived, in plain sRGB, as the concept's: bg-950 `space` 35% to black; bg-900, 800, 700 `space` with `ink` at 4.5%, 7.5%, 12%; line-strong and ink-ghost at 26%; ink-muted `dim` 42% to `ink`; accent-bright `live` halfway to `glow`; accent-dim `live` 38% to `space`.

Colour-blind safety: the concept measured working against needs you in OKLab under simulated colour blindness (`docs/concepts/art/README.md` on the `art-concepts` branch; its matrices were typed from memory): 0.22 normal, 0.15 deutan, 0.17 protan, 0.27 tritan, against mint and amber's 0.12 deutan. Marks don't rely on it: each state has its own shape.

## Type
- **Manrope** (variable, weight 500 almost everywhere, `ss01` and `tnum`). The wordmark uses weight 200.
- **JetBrains Mono** for terminals, hex ids, paths and numbers in meters.
- Labels: 10sp uppercase, tracking 0.18em, weight 600.
- Sizes: display 34, title 22, heading 16, body 15, small 13, mono 12/11.

## Atmosphere
- **One clock** (`fx/Ambient.kt`): the grain, the marks, pulsing dots and the terminal cursor all read a single `AmbientClock`; the chart reads none. It runs at 30fps (20 on battery saver), sleeps between frames instead of waking on every vsync, stops when the app isn't visible, and freezes under Reduce Motion. Animations read it only in draw/layer lambdas, so ticks never recompose. There are no infinite transitions anywhere.
- **The chart** (`fx/Atlas.kt`; owner, 2026-11-01, Atlas's quiet version): an engraved star chart behind every screen, where the old starfield showed. Line art on near-black.
  - **Grid:** a conic graticule round a pole 420dp above the screen: parallels every 44dp from r 360dp, meridians every 7.5° out to ±60°, ink at 15%, a 0.4dp line that never goes under a pixel.
  - **Milky Way:** one dot per 26 dp² of screen in a meandering band from lower left to upper right, in `light` at 22 to 34% (brighter in its middle), 0.6, 0.85 or 1.15dp square, with a dotted edge at ink 11%. Nothing in it means anything in this version: it isn't the budget.
  - **Field stars:** 120 on a 360 × 780dp phone, ink at 27 to 68%. **They thin with the week** (light = 1 − 0.65 × week^1.5, in 1/32 steps), from all of them to about a third; the leading agent's week, Claude Code's or Codex's. The only meaning it carries.
  - **It draws no frames at rest.** It reads no clock. It's `Canvas` calls (`drawPoints`, `drawLine`, `drawCircle`), no shaders, gradients or bitmaps of its own, drawn into one offscreen layer that's kept, once when it first shows and once for each step of the week (about 21 in a week). Only it feeds the edge bars' blur, as the far sky did. Reduce Motion changes nothing: nothing moves.
  - **The turn is in jumps.** The concept's slow turn would have meant 5 frames a second and hairlines shimmering by less than a pixel. Instead the pole sits in one of nine places, ±14dp, to a whole pixel, picked when the app comes to the front (a new one every 20 minutes), so the lines don't sit on the same OLED pixels for days. Not tested for burn-in.
  - **Behind a screen of text** (anything but Home: a chat, a file, onboarding) it steps back to 45%, as a layer's alpha, redrawing nothing, so the band isn't dense under a line of words.
  - Measured and reasoned: DECISIONS, "Atlas".
  - **The sky is gone** (owner, 2026-11-01; DECISIONS, "The sky is gone"). The sun, disk, planets, moons, Codex's star and the companion were hidden from Home on 2026-10-30 and deleted two days later, with their tests. What they said is on the usage and account cards. What's left of them: the phone's thud for a 5-hour limit (`fx/LimitFeel.kt`).
- **Marks** (`components/Marks.kt`, on the shared clock only while they move): a shape for each state as well as a colour, at their real sizes. **`Planet` in the code and "planet" in this file's older lines mean this mark now.**
  - **Working:** a solid diamond (r 2.6u) in two 120° arcs (r 5.5u, round caps) that turn once in 3 s on the calm pace. `u` is the mark's width over 14: 12dp `Planet`, 10dp in a chip.
  - **Needs you:** a larger diamond (r 3.3u) in four corner brackets, pulsing gently. On the filled amber chip, in the page's dark.
  - **Idle:** a hollow diamond (r 3.2u, line 1u), still, reading no clock.
  - **Reduce Motion:** the shapes stay and hold still. Other lit chips (signed out, an error): a solid diamond. Ended: a short rule. Blocked (`EclipseMark`): a hollow diamond struck through.
  - **The connection chip** (`MacMoon`, 20dp): a solid diamond while linked; two arcs turn round it after 0.4 s of looking; a hollow diamond struck through offline, a plain hollow one unpaired.
  - Statistics' projects are solid diamonds in a shade from their name, sized by hours. The voice capsule and the mic's settling state use the working mark's parts inside their rings (`turningStar`).
- **Grain** (`fx/Grain.kt`): grey noise with α ≤ 90/255, overlay blend, opacity 0.32, alive on every screen. It has a gentle lane of its own on the shared clock, 12 patterns a second (8 on a warm phone), whose frames redraw nothing, the sky and the blurred bars included (owner, 2026-10-17: it froze everywhere but Home after the heat work). A new pattern is the noise moved by a random whole number of cells, done by moving its layer, so only the frame is composited again.
  - Cells are 1.1dp: 0stems' ~1.5 CSS px at desktop distance, scaled for a phone held at half the distance.
  - Tiles are drawn with bilinear upscaling, like the browser does.
  - One 192² tile is generated once per process on a background thread, and drawn a tile larger than the screen so it can move.
  - The grain sits in its own layer (non-offscreen, so Overlay still blends), which means a tick never re-records the blurred content. It stays static on battery saver and on a hot phone.
- **Edges:** fully edge to edge, including the camera cutout, and locked to portrait.
  - Content scrolling under the status bar and the bottom bar is blurred with Haze: radius 22dp, bg tint 42%, full resolution.
  - The blur is at **full strength under the chrome itself** and fades to sharp only over the bar's last 35%. No black strips.
  - Each screen's content is a keyed source (`home`, `session:<id>`), and its bars blur only the chart plus their own screen. Without this, both screens would bleed into each other's bars during the cross-fade.
  - Android 10/11 have no RenderEffect, so a designed scrim with the same shape is drawn instead.

## Components
- **Seam:** a small planet, tracked label, a hairline (optional: the session's status row has none), and a trail. The planet is the activity meter: still at rest, spinning on its axis while live (meridians sliding across a sphere lit from the upper left), and amber, pulsing, when it needs you. Between states it eases: it slows to a stop and greys out, or brightens and spins up, and the label's colour follows. (It replaced three bars that read as an audio meter, then a moon on an orbit.) A trail of figures is drawn (stamps, count marks, a FitRow; see "Session metadata, drawn"); a trail in words is one short phrase.
- **GlassCard:** radius 16, a dark gradient at **78–86% opacity**, a 1dp inset ring at ink 8%, and 1dp highlight lines at the top (ink 25%) and bottom (ink 12%). A card that needs a look (amber for needs you, red for an error, mint for a calm limit) is never tinted (owner, 2026-10-18: "colour only as a thin accent"): a 2dp edge of its colour down its left side, brightest in the middle and fading toward the corners, a soft 40dp glow of it beside the edge inside the card, and the ring at 12% of it.
- **Attention card** (`components/Attention.kt`; Home's offline, account and provider cards, sessions whose provider was removed): the GlassCard with that thin edge, a 32dp glass circle holding the card's glyph in its colour (a broken link offline, a key for a refused key, a banknote for credit, a person for the account, a stopwatch for a rate limit, an eclipse for a limit), the title (heading) and one line (small, muted), a quiet figure under it when there is one (`⏱ resets in 3h 19m`, `retrying in 4s`), and the one action as a small Primary pill (36dp) on the right, or under the words at the end when its words are long. Other ways on are text links in accent-dim under the line, 18dp apart; a sign-in in progress grows the card below. **Swipe right** to put one away (left only gives 16dp, so it reads as the wrong way; a haptic tick at the 38% threshold): a slim glass line `HIDDEN  OpenRouter key refused  Undo` stands in for 6 s, then it's gone until what it says changes (another state, reason or reset brings it back; `data/Attention.kt`). TalkBack has Dismiss as an action. A sign-in in progress can't be put away.
  - *Deliberate deviation:* 0stems uses 22% fills behind a 40px backdrop blur. We skip per-card blur, which would mean a dozen live blurs over the background (an animated sky when this was decided, gone since), and use opacity to keep it out from behind card text.
- **Icons** (`components/Icons.kt`): check, cross, back, send, interrupt, plus and chevron are drawn as hairline strokes. Neither font carries ✓ or ⎋ (a system fallback would render them at the wrong weight). The arrows exist in both fonts, but they're drawn too so the set shares one stroke. The figures' glyphs are in the same set (see "Session metadata, drawn").
- **Pill:** every kind is frosted glass, the family of the state chips: a body a little lighter than what is under it, a light along the top edge, no outline. Primary is mint-tinted glass (faint mint body, mint edge light, mint glow at the glyph, mint label); Outline is neutral glass with an ink label; Ghost is fainter glass with a muted label; Danger is red-tinted glass with a red label; Glass is the dense pill in Home's top bar (New session, 30dp). No solid fills, no rings. Pressing moves it down 1dp, dims it and gives a haptic tick. Choices (mode chips, days, tiles, quick prompts, segmented controls, switches) use the shared `glassChoice`: neutral glass when off, washed and edge-lit in the accent when on. Cards, sheets, dialogs, tables and text fields keep their own surfaces and rings.
- **StatusChip:** a pill with a 5dp dot, and the dot pulses when live. "Needs you" uses the filled amber variant. A glyph can stand in the dot's place: a session a Mac restart ended says `MAC RESTARTED` behind the resume arrow (writing picks it up again), never "Mac restarted · resume".
- **Group seams** (Home; owner, 2026-10-29, as `claude agents` lists them): the sessions in three groups, `NEEDS INPUT` (amber, its planet pulsing), `WORKING` (its planet turning) and `COMPLETED` (idle or done, and what a restart of the Mac cut short; still), each a seam over its cards with its count as a **count mark** in the group's colour (`● 2`). A group with nothing in it has no seam. In Needs input and Working the cards go A to Z by name, so a card moves only when its group changes; Completed goes newest first (owner, 2026-10-30), by the time its stamp says. TalkBack hears each as a heading: "Working, 3 sessions."
- **Home search** (owner, 2026-10-31, idea HM8; `data/HomeSearch.kt`): a **lens** in Home's top bar before New session (Sessions only): a 30dp circle of dense frosted glass, the Glass pill's family, a 14dp search glyph in ink, in a 48dp target; lit mint (glyph and glow) while the search is open, when a tap closes it. TalkBack: "Search sessions", "Close search". Open, the house search field (the pickers' `SearchField`: a lens, `Search sessions`, the cross) sits under the bar inside its blur, 16dp from the edges, and the list starts 52dp lower; the keyboard comes up. The cross, Back or leaving the tab closes it and clears the words. Typing filters the three groups by title, project, the folder's own name, last line and short hex id, any case, as History's search does: the groups keep their order, their counts are the matches, an empty group hides. From 3 characters (300 ms after typing pauses) the server's full-text hits on Home's sessions join them, and such a card shows the snippet under its title (mono small, muted, the words in the accent, 2 lines at most) in place of a plain last line (an ask, a block or what a Working card does stays under it). While words are typed the cards above the sessions step aside (the offline card stays); History's dim lines say "Searching inside conversations…" and "Partial results…", and nothing found is a dim `No sessions match`.
- **Connection chip** (Home's brand row): the Mac's mark, its name, then its battery drawn, a small battery (15 × 8dp hairline body and nub, filled to its level; amber under 20% on battery, red under 10%) and the percentage (`MacBook Air ▭ 62%`, a mint bolt while it charges), then the route as a 12dp glyph: Wi-Fi's fan on the Mac's own network, a globe over a remote address. No "MacBook Air · 62%", no "· Local"; TalkBack still hears "Connected to MacBook Air, battery 62%, charging, over Local". A tap opens the **switcher**. ## Widgets
One home screen widget (`.../hypernova/widget/`, beside `ui/`), **Needs you** (owner, 2026-10-31; docs/DECISIONS.md, "The widget: Needs you"): every session that needs the owner on every paired computer, grouped by computer, with Deny and Allow right on the widget. It replaced the two of 2026-10-15, the small sun and the large sessions widget. Its class keeps the name `SessionsWidget`, so a placed large widget stays; a placed sun disappears.

- **What RemoteViews allowed.** The launcher draws a widget, not the app (plain RemoteViews; Glance would only have added WorkManager), and that shaped the look:
  - **No app fonts.** Layouts inflate in a restricted context, so the system sans stands in at Manrope's weights (labels still tracked caps at 0.18em) and monospace for numbers and commands. The tokens are repeated as resources (`values/widget.xml`).
  - **No pictures.** Text, the card, the command well and the pills, in the app's colours: no sun, planets, meters or grain (the owner: remove the images, a plain clean look).
  - **No countdowns.** Nothing redraws a widget every minute, so times are clock times (`Last updated 14:32`), which stay true until they pass.
  - **One layout, a list that scrolls.** A ListView whose rows come from `WidgetListService` (made from the picture last drawn, so the header and the list agree), at any size; nothing is measured to decide what fits. A list's rows share one PendingIntent (a template each row fills in), and a row both opens and answers, so every tap goes through `WidgetTap`, an activity that never shows (`Theme.NoDisplay`): it opens the app on the row's computer at its session, or hands Deny and Allow to `WidgetActions`, whose broadcast keeps the process for the round trip.
- **Card:** the near-black at 92% (a wallpaper is busier than the chart) with a 1dp ink-8% ring, the launcher's own corner radii on Android 12+. Rows are a faint ink fill (4%) with a hairline ring (ink 8%), the launcher's inner radius, 6dp apart, a soft ink ripple when pressed.
- **Header:** `NEEDS YOU` in tracked caps (ink muted, 10sp), the count in amber mono, a hairline, and `Last updated 14:32` (dim mono) while nothing keeps the widget current. A tap opens the app.
- **Computers:** each computer with something waiting has a line: its name in tracked caps (dim), its count in amber, and `Offline` after it while it can't be reached. In the switcher's order (the one on screen first, then as paired). A computer with nothing waiting is left out. A tap opens the app on that computer.
- **Rows** (`WidgetModel.of`, unit-tested): one per session that needs you. Its name (`+2 more` when other approvals wait behind its oldest), what it waits on in small amber caps with the project after it in dim caps (the tool, `BASH`; Claude Code's dialog in the terminal by its title, `BASH COMMAND`; `QUESTION`; `PLAN TO REVIEW`; `WAITING FOR YOU` for anything else), then the detail: a command in the mono well with an amber `$` (`›` for other tools), or the question, the plan's first line or what it waits on as prose (up to 3 lines). Oldest wait first: an approval by when it was asked, a dialog in the terminal or another wait by when its session started, so the list holds still. A tap opens the session, switching to its computer.
- **Buttons** (pills 32dp in 44dp touch targets): a tool's approval and Claude Code's dialog in the terminal get **Deny**; **Allow** only beside the whole command (the notifications' rule, `WidgetModel.answerable`: exactly as it will run, its own line breaks, nothing masked or cut, at most 400 characters), which the row then shows whole; otherwise **Open** beside Deny. Questions, plans and other waits get Open. The dialog's Deny and Allow press its last No and its Yes through the menu route, as a Home card's. A tap shows `Allowing…` or `Denying…` in place of the buttons, then the row goes, or says `Didn't go through` and the buttons come back. On a locked phone (a lock-screen widget) nothing is answered: the session opens after unlocking, as a notification's buttons need the phone unlocked first.
- **Empty:** one quiet line in the list's place: `Nothing needs you` (with `Can't reach z2` under it while a computer is offline, or `Updates while Hypernova is open`), `Connecting…` before any computer is heard from, or `No computer paired` and `Open Hypernova to pair`.
- **Stale never looks fresh.** The widget is live only while the app is on screen or the background connection runs. Otherwise it says `Last updated 14:32` (a weekday or a date once it's older), stamped with when a computer was last heard, not when it was redrawn, and an approval past the moment it leaves the phone for nobody answering goes from the list. A computer not heard from yet in a freshly started process shows what was kept for it, dimmed, to open only; an offline computer's rows only open too, dimmed.
  - A live picture checks back every half hour while the phone is in use: if the system has killed the app in the meantime, the widget is redrawn as `Last updated …` rather than staying live-looking.
- **Updates:** the widget follows every paired computer's hub (the one on screen and the ones watched in the background), also when no screen of the app is open (the background connection, or a process started just for the widget). A change to what needs you on any of them redraws at most every 1.5s, a burst drawn once with the state as it is by then. There's no redraw timer and no update period: nothing changes, nothing is drawn. A picture that goes out of date by itself (an approval leaving the phone, the day changing under `Last updated`) is redrawn by a non-waking alarm, and nothing is drawn while the screen is off (the newest state waits for it to come on).
- **Nothing secret on the home screen.** Titles, activity lines and commands are masked with the daemon's patterns for session titles (API keys and tokens, long random runs), plus what a command line carries: the value after a name like `API_KEY=`, `TOKEN:`, `--password` or `Bearer`, a URL's password, and curl's `-u`. A command that needed masking gets Deny and Open, never Allow. The widget masks and shows at most the first 600 characters of a text (an ask's 400 and 200 more), cut back to a space, and in a curl or wget command reads a quoted string whole up to that length, so a long JSON body with `&` in it never ends the scan before a `-u`. TalkBack reads no further than what was masked.
- **Preview:** on Android 12+ the widget picker draws `widget_preview` (the widget with generic sample data and no clock time); before that it shows the app's icon. The debug SAMPLE and RENDER broadcasts went with the sun.

- **No faint light.** The lens lights level 1 of 15 at about 37% perceived brightness, so nothing glows or fades below that: shapes are solid, shading lives between about 40% and full, and small shapes are quantised crisply instead of dithered into a speckled halo. Pictures are drawn as perceived brightness and mapped through the lens' curve (`src/lens/gray.ts`).
- **Motion is rationed,** since every frame is a Bluetooth write. Frames go out only while something moves, at most about three a second, never ahead of text. After 90s without news (the lens showing something new, from the Mac or a gesture; the clock turning "2m" into "3m" isn't news), one resting frame (bracket closed, sun steady, meridians at ±45°) and then only the clock's text, about once a minute. New text in a box is one small write; anything that moves a box rebuilds the page.
- **Telescope** fills the lens: one label line (`TELESCOPE   hypernova   emulator`, with `just now`, the zoomed quarter or "watching" on the right) over a 576×256 picture in four 288×128 tiles. A light picture is turned dark, contrast is stretched, the brightest parts are kept below full, and the rest is error-diffused to the 16 levels, tile by tile, so a change in one part of the picture costs one tile (a tile in which almost nothing moved keeps its pixels). The terminal is drawn for the lens by the Mac (#0F0D0A ground, ink, muted, dim and ghost greys, mint for Claude's ⏺ and the prompt, danger red for errors, JetBrains Mono) and goes to the nearest levels without dithering, so its text keeps clean edges. While there's no picture, its place is a framed box with the reason, and the gestures are the quiet line at the foot.
- **Pop-ups** are a framed two-line band over the bottom of the page. What it covers steps aside: a box under it goes, and one that reaches into it loses its hidden lines. Over Telescope's full-screen picture the band is the top line instead, so no tile is dropped. In moving mode it is a chime and one line.
- **The battery card** ("battery", or a swipe up from the glance's first line): drawn glasses and laptop icons with 5-segment bars (one segment per started fifth) in one small image, and the name and percent as text (`62%   charging`, `low`). A Mac without a battery, or one that is away, isn't listed. Stay awake's state shows under the Mac as phrases ("Awake while working", "Awake with the lid closed until 18:30", "Sleeps at 14:02: running hot", "Can sleep: Backpack needs its setup on the Mac", "Sleeps as usual"). The glance has no battery line.
- **Transmission** (text arriving like speech): boxes keep their size while words arrive, so each step is one text update, in steps of up to 3 words that end early at punctuation or a line break. The pause after a step is 0.6 of a word at a comma, 1.4 at a full stop and 2.2 at a paragraph; steps are never closer than 0.3s. Text already on screen when the live view opens shows at once. A finished reply arrives the whole way through its scrolling text, the window following the newest line one line at a time; a swipe back stops it, and the end follows again.
- **Long text scrolls, never pages.** A reply, a plan read whole and the diff are a window onto one text. The window is as tall as the text needs, up to what the card holds over its facts, and stays the same size however far it's scrolled, so a swipe or a step of words arriving is one text update. A swipe moves one line less than the window, so the last line in view becomes the first. A ↓ at the right end of the last line says more follows, and ↑ says there is more above (the text is wrapped 27px narrower, so a mark never cuts a line). The firmware's own scrollbar isn't used: it jumps back to the top on every text change.
- **Dictation and the send countdown.** After the temple is let go the captions stay and settle (`getting the last words…`), then the prompt shows whole in the same window, a long one opening at its end with the ↑/↓ marks. The top line then reads `sending in 2` and counts down; the footer says `tap send now · double tap cancel`. A swipe stops the count. If the last words are late, the top line says `last words missing?`, there is no count, and it waits for a tap. SENDING and SENT keep the same lines on screen.
- **Moving mode** is two short lines at the top-left and nothing else: no planets, so no animation frames. On the glance, the line in focus plus `1 of 4   14:05   42%`; on a card, its head and the one thing to do next. A word the lens says, such as "Allowed", takes the second line.
- **The diff** is one text that scrolls through every file. Each file after the first begins with `── PairTest.kt   +3 -0` on a line of its own; the header names the file at the top, and a tap brings the next file there. Each change is marked "… line 41". + and - sit in their own column, since in the firmware font a plus, a hyphen and two spaces are equally wide. Indentation is halved, and long lines wrap at a bracket or a dot. At most 80 lines a file, then "… more on the phone".
- **The guide on the glasses:** a card per step (`GUIDE 1 of 5   TAP`, the gestures a gap apart), the right gesture ticked with what it does, a wrong one named.
- **Eclipse** (do not disturb) shows in the glance's corner as `eclipse   42%`, kept apart from the sun's eclipse (`signed out`).
- **Quiet by default.** The glance shows only the sessions that matter (working, needing you, or finished in the last 45 minutes; four at most). Double tap and the lens goes dark; only something that needs you brings it back.
- **States the glance says in the phone's words** (Lens 0.1.11). The Mac away: `offline since 08:15` in the corner and the rows at rest as it last said them, their ages stopped. Hypernova on the phone no longer answering: a band, `HYPERNOVA STOPPED` over `Open Hypernova on your phone`. A session stopped while you were away: `paused while you were away` where the row has no room for the phone's `paused after 10 minutes without an answer` (a tap on its card continues it). Another provider's trouble on the row (`OpenRouter is out of credits`, `… refused its key`, `… is rate limited until 14:05`), popping up once as a band. A needs card or band says `auto-deny in 9m` (a terminal menu, how long it waited). A mode that isn't the usual shows on the live view and card (`plan`, `accept edits`, `auto`, `bypass`, `allowed only`).
- **Connecting** is four plain screens on the same text: `Allow it on your phone` with the six-digit code on a line of its own, `Allowed on your phone?` (tap to connect, or allow it in Hypernova's own prompt), `Not confirmed on the glasses` (tap to ask again), and `Update Hypernova Lens` when the phone's app needs a newer Lens. The tap on the glasses keeps the access for 10 minutes.
- **Type on the lens** (`lens/src/lens/look.ts`; Settings: Built-in, the default since 2026-10-18, or Hypernova: drawn pictures were too slow on real glasses, LATER L57). Hypernova is a hybrid: the glance (and a pop-up over it) and approvals are drawn on the phone in the app's own type and sent as up to four image tiles, only those whose levels changed (`drawn.ts`, `painter.ts`, `tiles.ts`); replies (arriving, finished, scrolled), the live view and every other screen keep the glasses' font, where a new line or a swipe is one small text update rather than four tiles (measured: about 25 times the bytes while a reply streams). `?look=all` (development only, never in Settings) draws replies too, for trying them on the glasses (LATER L57). Words in Manrope 15px (13px the least: at 12px "e" closes up on the lens), commands in JetBrains Mono 13px (12px the least), a seam's label in 11px tracked caps; glyph edges go to the nearest level with the faintest fringe off, never dithered, and a thin stroke of dim text stays lit. The seam is a tracked label, the project in semibold, a hairline and the trail in mono; the needs-you label is the one filled block; the line in focus sits in a drawn ring; a command sits in a terminal's hairline frame after its prompt; a hint is the gestures' glyphs (tap one ring, double tap two dots, swipe a double arrow, hold a dot in two rings) and what they do. The glance keeps its planets as their own 32×144 image beside three tiles (a frame costs what it does natively) and leaves the bottom right dark; an approval or a band takes four 288×144 tiles, its planet at rest.
- **Spending on the drawn glance is a projection meter** (the owner's projection meters, `core/meter.ts`), not the text corner: `5H` in tracked caps and the percentage in mono, an 88×7 bar (the track a hairline, used solid, the pace a fainter fill to where Usage.rate leads by the reset, only while something works), then one tag: when the limit comes before the reset, how soon ("47m", bright) beside a notch standing out of the bar's end; else the reset, dim, after a drawn circular-arrow glyph. At a limit the bar is full with the notch and the tag is when it lifts; a model's week, while it is the tightest, is a plain meter under its name (`FABLE`); eclipse puts `ECLIPSE` before it. Signed out, no plan, on credits and offline keep the corner's words. Built-in keeps the text line.
- **The phone screen** inside the Even App is the full web version of the look (`src/phone/`, the skill's `hyper-cosmos.css` and `cosmos.js`): the living sky, Manrope and JetBrains Mono, glass cards, and a live mirror of the lens in the G2's green at each level's real brightness.

## The offline queue

A prompt typed while the Mac is away (Connecting, Reconnecting or Offline, or a send that got no answer) is not refused: it waits on the phone (`data/OfflineQueue.kt`, kept in the app's preferences, so it survives the app closing and the phone restarting; only the prompt's text and the paths of files already on the Mac).

- In the chat it is the user's own bubble, quiet, with a drawn laptop glyph and "Waiting for your Mac", and one line: "Sends itself when your Mac is back". Long-press: Edit, Copy, Remove. The composer's hint says "Your Mac is away. Prompts wait."
- Home's session card carries a "N waiting" chip (laptop glyph) beside the state chip.
- When the socket opens again the prompts go oldest first, one at a time, each under its own id as the send's `clientId`; the daemon runs one `clientId` once within 10 minutes (PROTOCOL.md, "POST /v1/sessions/{id}/prompt"), so a repeat after a lost answer never doubles. The bubble becomes the normal Sending/Sent one. A session that can't take it yet (an approval is open, the terminal is busy) is tried again after 20 s, a few times.
- A session the Mac no longer has: the prompt stays ("This session ended", "Start a new session with it" on the bubble and its menu; also a card on Home, since the session's own screen is gone) and starts a session in the same folder.
- New attachments can't upload while the Mac is away: the file's chip is marked and says so; the text stays in the box. Files uploaded before the Mac went away go with the prompt.
- New session while the Mac is away: the simplest honest behaviour. The sheet says "Your Mac is away. Start waits for it; your words stay here." and Start is disabled; the draft is kept as it always was. A new session is not queued.
